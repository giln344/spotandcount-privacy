Privacy Policy for Spot & Count

App: Spot & Count (on Google Play as "Spot & Count: Find Differences", app ID com.blinkwise.spotandcount)

Developer: Blinkwise Games

Effective date: October 2026

Last updated: 2026-10-03

This privacy policy explains what happens to your information when you play Spot & Count. The short version: we do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. Everything the game saves stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Spot & Count does not collect, send, sell or share personal data or any other information about you or your device.

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • No ads. There are no ads, no advertising ID and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or crash reporting. There are no tracking or analytics tools in the app.
  • No purchases. The coins in the game are earned by playing. There is no real money in the app.
  • No internet use. The game itself does not connect to the internet. Its pictures, 3D models, sounds and font are all inside the app, and it works fully offline.

What is saved on your phone

To make the game work, the app saves a few small things only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • your best scores and best times,
  • your Daily Challenge results and streak,
  • your settings (sound and vibration, colour marks),
  • your progress: player level, coins, stars on the map, shop items, achievements and records.

This information does not leave your phone through the app, and we cannot see it.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

  • The saved game data stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • To delete it, uninstall the app, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Spot & Count → Storage → Clear storage (the names differ a little between phones).
  • If your phone's Android backup is turned on, Android may include the game's saved data in your own device backup in your Google account and put it back if you install the game again. Android and Google handle that backup under your account; we cannot see it. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

Rating the game

  • After you finish a world, the game may ask Google Play to show its own rating window. That window belongs to Google Play, not to the game. If you leave a rating or review, Google receives it under Google's own privacy policy. The game itself never sees or receives your rating.

Sharing your results

  • The Share buttons (Daily Challenge and "Share my week") make a short text, for example "Spot & Count Daily #15 🐻 5,770 pts 🟩🟩🟩🟨🟩". The text has no name, no ID and no link. The app copies it to your phone's clipboard (or opens your phone's share menu, where available). You choose if and where to paste or send it. Nothing is sent to us.

The backup code

  • The backup code (Settings & backup) is a text made from your saved progress, so you can move it to another phone yourself. It is shown only on your phone. If you tap Copy, it goes to your clipboard; you decide where to keep it.
  • Restore only reads a code that you paste into the box yourself. The app never reads your clipboard on its own.
  • We never receive your backup code.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for small buzzes during the game. One tap on the speaker button turns sound and vibration off.
  • Internet: apps built with our app framework (Capacitor) include this permission by default. Spot & Count does not use it: the game does not send or receive any data.

The app does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Security

Because the app sends nothing over the internet and has no servers, your game data is never transmitted and never stored by us. It is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

Children

Spot & Count is made for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information (for example, if we ever add optional online features such as Google Play Games leaderboards), we will update this page and the dates above before that version of the app is released, and say clearly what changes.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact Blinkwise Games at: blinkwisegames@gmail.com
